# Automesh 0.5.31

Closed groups support attachments and stickers. Members can download a file
from the sender or another eligible member who has a verified copy. A node
can relay the encrypted message and its attachment card. Downloading the
file itself requires a direct connection to a member who has it.

Group members exchange signed nicknames during group synchronization.
Type `@` to find a member by nickname. Suggestions for matching nicknames
show the member's avatar and the last eight characters of their ID.
The selected member sees an unread `@` beside the group until they read
the message.
