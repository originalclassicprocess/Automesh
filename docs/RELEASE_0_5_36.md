# Automesh 0.5.36

Hold a conversation in the chat list to pin, unpin, or delete it. A right
click or the context-menu keyboard shortcut opens the same actions. Pins
are saved locally for personal chats, groups, LoRa channels and the AI chat.
Deleting a group leaves it and clears its local history. The owner chooses a
successor when other participants remain.

The node queue opens messages by conversation and separates your group
messages from relays for other participants. It shows the node's actual
envelope count and storage usage. Incoming files can continue downloading
through a separate authenticated node when your configured node is offline.
